Reports spaces or TABs at the end of a payload line of a vault envelope (ANS-V103). ansible-vault concatenates the payload lines without stripping them, so the blanks become part of the hex and decryption fails with Odd-length string or Non-hexadecimal digit found. Editors and scripts that pad lines (for example an awk step of a rotation script) can produce them.

Quick fix: Strip trailing whitespace removes the blanks from every line of the value. Nothing is decrypted or re-encrypted.
