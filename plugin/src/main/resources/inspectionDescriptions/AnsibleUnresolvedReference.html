Reports static references that ansible-core cannot resolve and fails on (ANS-R001):

  • include_tasks/import_tasks targets and tasks_from, handlers_from, vars_from, defaults_from of include_role/import_role;
  • template and copy src files, searched like ansible-core does (<role>/templates or <role>/files, the role, the task file's directory);
  • notify names that no handler answers to (by name, listen topic or role : name), looking at the own role, every play that applies it, and the rest of the root;
  • roles (plays, includes, meta/main.yml dependencies, molecule's /ansible/roles/<role>) when the role search path is limited to project directories by roles_path;
  • vars_files, import_playbook and {% include %} in role templates.

Not reported: a copy with a truthy remote_src, templated values, absolute paths outside the project that no container mapping covers (/etc/…, /tmp/…), and names that may exist at runtime (templated handler names, roles the project cannot find).

The quick fix changes the value to the nearest existing name, for example tasks_from: configure to configuration. The severity follows the root's Ansibility settings.
